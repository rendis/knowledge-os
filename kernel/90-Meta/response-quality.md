# Evidence-backed answers

Apply before delivering a material conclusion from retrieved or reinterpreted evidence, whether conversational, operational or retained. Keep the initiating workflow and its access/publication gates. Investigation here means evidence gathering, not a requirement to create a case. Review effort is part of answer quality; token savings do not waive it.

## Review or reuse

- **New conclusion:** prepare the answer, then check it against the rubric below. Use a distinct author verification pass for bounded, low-impact analysis outside the independent-review conditions below. Read-only access, a short answer and the absence of a requested action do not make a conclusion low impact.
- **Operational decision or material uncertainty:** obtain independent acceptance before delivering classifications of operational status, eligibility, enablement or failure causes; also require it when a conclusion could justify an operational intervention or closure, sources conflict, or correctness depends on uncertain relationships across components. Classify by the meaning of the answer, not whether the user requested execution. Give the reviewer the question, candidate answer, relevant source evidence and scope, not just the author's summary. The reviewer checks both unsupported assertions and omitted conclusions that the available evidence can establish. It inspects the decisive implementation or permitted deterministic result when exact behavior matters, without repeating the full investigation. The author, including a subagent, requests this review and waits for acceptance before delivering the conclusion. The reviewer returns specific unsupported claims or acceptance against the rubric without delegating another review.
- **Unavailable reviewer:** honor explicit executor restrictions. When no stricter workflow requires independent acceptance, perform a separate author check, disclose the missing independent review briefly, and bound the answer to supported facts and unresolved implications. Withhold the newly required operational classification or recommendation until independent acceptance; report literal/source-supported observations and the unresolved classification instead. Publication gates remain mandatory.
- **Previously reviewed conclusion:** check correspondence and freshness only when the reviewed claims, evidence, entity, environment, period and intended use remain applicable, with no new inference or contradiction. Locate the earlier review and its evidence in available context or authorized records; a prior answer or user agreement alone is not a review. If review evidence is unavailable or scope changes, review the affected conclusions anew. Reuse unaffected work.

A review needs no new file, case or public checklist. Retain enough claim/evidence/scope and review context in the existing workflow to justify reuse; if that context is lost, the reuse exception is unavailable. An accepted source or note review can cover identical answer claims, but additions and new implications require their own check.

## Rubric

| Check | Acceptance condition |
| --- | --- |
| Support | Each decision-relevant assertion is supported by inspected evidence; observations, grounded inferences and proposals are distinguishable. A citation supports the exact claim, not merely its topic. A user's assertion that evidence is absent does not replace inspecting relevant supplied sources. |
| Correspondence | Entity, environment, provider, component, revision/time and the user's question match. Preserve the purpose of each input: a screenshot used for identifiers is not proof of a reported failure. |
| Sufficiency | Evidence establishes the claimed effect. A stored flag or uncalled query does not prove process control; trace the relevant caller and conditions. Until that use is established, report the literal value and its unknown operational effect; avoid activation/status labels inferred from the field name or boolean value. Configuration, execution, acceptance and persisted outcome remain distinct. Check exceptions or alternatives that could change the conclusion. Missing search results are bounded observations, not universal absence. An external evidence gap does not excuse leaving locally decidable behavior unresolved: distinguish each field, input shape, processing stage and scope needed by the question, then state the remaining unknown. |
| Continuity | Preserve established facts and resolved flow identity; identify only the remaining gap. Explicitly retract prior claims invalidated by new evidence. Establish a hypothesis's connection to this case before using it as an explanation. |
| Clarity | The intended reader can find the answer, understand its meaning and use it without overinterpreting certainty. Explain necessary terms and retain decisive evidence and limits; omit repetition and investigation narration. |

A defect requires a targeted correction or the smallest authorized missing check. Recheck the changed claims and their dependent conclusions. For delegated work, use the bounded repair and escalation policy in [execution profiles](execution-profiles.md); this review adds no separate retry allowance. For author-only review, stop the affected conclusion when the same defect survives one focused correction. Unavailable required evidence/access or work beyond authorized scope also stops that conclusion. Deliver supported findings and the precise limitation instead. Continue necessary authorized evidence gathering and reuse accepted sources; distinct findings do not justify an unbounded review cycle.

## Communication

Lead with the answer. Use a professional, formal tone, direct sentences and familiar, concrete language that the intended reader can understand. Explain technical terms when the reader needs them. Give only the reasoning, source references and uncertainty necessary to understand and use the conclusion. Expand for consequential complexity; a short answer must not hide a decisive condition. Never replace an available necessary check with confident wording or an unsupported assumption.

These communication guidelines are informed by the relevance, findability, understandability and usability principles of [ISO 24495-1:2023](https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html); they are not a claim of formal conformity. Clear writing and review reduce risk, not guarantee truth. Validate behavior using blind evidence-based scenarios, including users who do not ask corrective questions.
