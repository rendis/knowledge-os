How a request becomes a trustworthy answer

A compact architecture for moving from a real question to a decision someone can inspect, challenge, and use.

01 / THE SHAPE OF THE WORK

Start with the question that has consequences.

Good explanations make the route visible. They name the question, show the evidence that can answer it, and leave uncertainty where it belongs.

A useful answer is rarely a single fact dropped into a blank page. It is a short chain of choices: what was asked, what was observed, what was inferred, and what remains unresolved.

02 / THE READER'S HANDLE

Give the reader a handle, then let them look closer.

Once the chain is explicit, a reader can disagree precisely. They can question the input, inspect the method, or accept the result while keeping one open item in view.
